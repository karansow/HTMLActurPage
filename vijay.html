Vijay

• Born: June 22, 1974, in Madras (now Chennai), India. • Family: Son of film director S. A. Chandrasekhar and playback singer Shoba Chandrasekhar; married to Sangeetha Sornalingam with two children. • Full Article: Read the detailed biography on the C. Joseph Vijay Wikipedia Page. Acting Career • Tenure: Began as a child artist before making his lead acting debut in Naalaiya Theerpu (1992). • Stardom: Gained massive popularity starring in major blockbusters like Ghilli, Thuppakki, Mersal, and Master. • Retirement: Formally announced his retirement from cinema in February 2024 to transition into full-time politics.
